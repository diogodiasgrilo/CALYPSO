## Part 4: New Learnings for Strategy Memory

**W48-specific, data-grounded, non-generic learnings:**

**1. VIX compression from Regime 3 to Regime 2 on large gap-up mornings can be 4+ VIX points by cash open, shifting the entire regime assignment:** Nov 23 pre-market VIX was 30.94 (Regime 3) but cash-open VIX printed at 26.76 (Regime 2) — a 4.18pt compression on a +2.94% / +329pt gap-up. This triggered the full 2-entry base schedule (not just 1), the Z2 buffer override ($1.00/$1.50), and 3 total entries under Variant B's PM grid. Apollo's pre-market regime forecast (Regime 3 modal) was overridden by the compression. For gap-ups ≥200pt / ≥2.5%, the operative VIX regime at the first entry slot should be treated as highly uncertain until the cash open prints; Apollo should bracket between pre-market regime and cash-open-compressed regime as co-equal scenarios rather than assigning one as the modal base case.

**2. Regime 3 entries can produce full expiry at credit when intraday V-reversals restore SPX above short puts that were breached earlier in the session:** Nov 24 short put at 7,160 was breached by 19.29pt intraday (session low 7,140.71) yet expired clean at the 7,198 close. The entry survived not because of initial cushion (1.57pt above open) but because SPX recovered 57.29pt from the session low by close. At 7c and $455 total credit, this is a structurally fragile outcome — a 10pt worse session low or a failure to recover would have triggered the put stop. HYDRA's current Z3 mechanics (MKT-020/022 tightening pulls short puts close to open in Regime 3) do not distinguish "recovered V" days from "continued drift" days — the entry fired with appropriate parameters but survived only on path-dependency.

**3. The Thanksgiving Eve early-close (1:00 PM ET) is a confirmed structural near-blackout for Variant B's anomalous 13:45/14:15/14:45 PM schedule — only the 12:15 slot falls within the trading window, and GEX adjuster or credit conditions will kill it on most days:** Nov 27 had no other viable slots. Even with Z2 conditions (VIX 26.17, SPX 7,265) and credits clearing MKT-011 floors ($27.50/$20.00 theoretical), the GEX adjuster produced a full skip. Early-close days (Thanksgiving Eve, Christmas Eve, etc.) where HYDRA's B-schedule runs in its anomalous 13:45+ configuration should be pre-classified as structural blackouts in Apollo's briefings, not RED-with-trade-possibility — the probability of any entry completing is structurally near-zero.

**4. Brandon TP@80% on extreme-credit-skew entries (put credit >85% of total) locks realized P&L before intraday continuation risks materialize:** Nov 25 (put credit 91.3% of total, $735/$805) achieved TP@80% on a session with a 228pt intraday range — the same session where SPX had already touched 6,971 (154pt below the 7,125 short put) before reversing. The TP mechanism fired during the rally phase after the reversal, correctly closing before any risk of reversal-of-reversal. In sessions where put credit exceeds 85% of total IC credit (a signal of extreme downside fear), TP@80% acts as a mandatory risk-management gate that should be retained regardless of subsequent session direction — the alternative (hold to expiry) would have been safe on Nov 25 but could have been catastrophic on a re-break.

**5. Regime 3 (VIX ≥28) sessions under Variant B's PM schedule with single late-afternoon entries (14:47–14:48 ET) produce a structurally distinct risk/reward profile from canonical multi-entry Regime 3 sessions:** Both Nov 24 (1 entry, $455 credit, expired clean) and Nov 25 (1 entry, $805 credit, TP@80%) demonstrate that single Regime 3 PM-grid entries placed after 14:45 have 60–75 minutes of remaining session time, concentrate all capital in a single IC with no stop-averaging across slots, and rely entirely on the market NOT making a sustained directional move in the final hour. W48 survived this structure cleanly, but the Nov 24 near-miss (put breached 19pt, recovered) illustrates the inherent binary nature of late-session single entries in Z3: winning days look clean; losing days would generate maximum-loss outcomes with no diversification offset from other slots.
