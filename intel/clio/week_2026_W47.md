With two Z1→Z2 crossing events each generating put stops, the direction of the effect is consistent: **Z1 put buffer of $2.50 may be too lenient when VIX is trending toward the 22 boundary.** Consider: change `vix_regime.put_stop_buffer[1]` (Z1) from `$2.50` to `$2.00`, or add a mid-session buffer re-override trigger when VIX crosses the Z1→Z2 boundary (22.0) intraday.
