## Part 3: Strategy Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Refine GEX Strike Adjuster to Flag "Clear-But-Into-Cluster" Placements

**Data basis:** Sep 25 Entry #3 (10:47) cleared the GEX adjuster and placed with short call at 7,750. HERMES confirms this placed the short call within the documented 7,730–7,760 GEX acceleration cluster that the GEX adjuster explicitly identifies. The entry was not skipped by the adjuster — it "cleared" — but then the intraday rally to 7,751.82 breached the 7,750 strike, triggering the call stop ($835 debit, $490 credit collected = −$345 pre-commission loss). Sep 21 shows the same pattern: 3 call stops on entries whose short calls were placed in the 7,735–7,760 cluster zone.

**Root cause:** The GEX adjuster currently has a binary output: SKIP (if strike is inside acceleration zone) or KEEP/SHIFT (if outside). But "KEEP" does not distinguish between "comfortably outside cluster" and "just outside cluster ceiling." An entry placed with short call at 7,750 when the cluster ceiling is 7,760 is 10pt from the danger zone; the expected-move buffer on a 5pt spread does not provide meaningful protection against a 10pt adverse move.

**Proposed config change:** Consider adding a `gex_proximity_warning_pts` parameter (e.g., 15pt) to `strategy.brandon.gex`. When a post-adjuster short call strike is within `gex_proximity_warning_pts` of the nearest GEX acceleration cluster boundary, the entry should either (a) be skipped (conservative) or (b) be logged with `close_reason_note: "cluster_proximity"` so the defensive overlay triggers earlier. Concrete form: `"gex_proximity_skip_pts": 15` — skip any call-side placement where the short call is within 15pt of a documented cluster boundary.

**Expected impact:** Sep 25 Entry #3 (7,750 short call, 10pt from 7,760 cluster ceiling) would have been skipped, avoiding −$345 realized loss (pre-commission). Sep 21's three call stops (short calls 7,735–7,760) would depend on precise cluster boundary placement at time of entry — not calculable without per-entry strike data, but HERMES confirms all three were placed "near 7,735–7,760" which overlaps the cluster zone.

**Confidence:** MEDIUM. The cluster boundary data comes from Polygon GEX and is already being used by the adjuster. The proximity threshold requires calibration — 15pt is illustrative; the actual threshold should be derived from the distribution of "cleared adjuster but stopped" entries.
