### Recommendation 2: Flag Gap-Up + Same-Day Reversal Pattern as Elevated Put-Stop Risk for E#2 Placement

**Data cited:** Jun 24 — SPX opened +101pt gap-up (7,377.06), surged to 7,427.45, then reversed to low of 7,337.70 (89.75pt range). E#2 placed at 11:20 with short put at 7,350 — only 12.70pt above session low. E#2 put stopped at $2,330 debit vs. $280 credit. E#1 placed at 10:45 with short put at 7,325 — survived with the same reversal (12.70pt to low, just barely). The Jun 8 W24 learning documented the same mechanism: E#2 on a gap-up/fade day places puts progressively closer to price as the reversal is underway, because SPX is at or near its intraday high at 11:15 entry time.

**Mechanism:** On gap-up opens that then fade, SPX is typically near its intraday high during the 11:15 E#2 entry window. MKT-024 places puts 2.75× OTM from the current (elevated) SPX level, but the "current level" is the session high, not the open. When SPX subsequently reverses to or below the open, the E#2 put strike — placed relative to the gap-up high — has far less cushion than expected relative to the actual intraday range.

**Recommendation (MEDIUM confidence):** Consider implementing a put-strike floor anchored to SPX open (not current SPX) when SPX at E#2 entry time is ≥0.5% above the 9:30 open. Specifically: `effective_put_strike ≤ min(MKT-024_calculated_strike, spx_open − put_otm_target)` — preventing the put from being placed above the open on a gap-up day. This would have placed Jun 24's E#2 put at ~7,300 (77pt below the 7,377 open) instead of 7,350 (27pt below the open-equivalent level), giving 37pt of cushion to the session low instead of 12.70pt. At 7,300, the stop would not have been triggered (low was 7,337.70). **Estimated impact on Jun 24:** avoids the entire $2,173.55 loss. Caveat: lower put strike also means lower put credit, potentially making the entry non-viable at Regime 1 floors.

**Config change phrasing:** This requires a new config parameter — e.g., `strategy.gap_day_put_anchor_enabled: true` with `strategy.gap_day_threshold_pct: 0.005` — rather than a simple existing-parameter adjustment. Flag as medium-term code change.
