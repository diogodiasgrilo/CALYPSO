## Part 3: Strategy Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Evaluate lowering `put_only_max_vix` from 15.0 to 16.5 in Regime 0

**Data basis:** On 05-28 E#2, VIX was 16.0. Call credit ($0.70) fell below the $1.00 Regime 0 floor. MKT-032 evaluated the put-only fallback but found VIX at 16.0 > 15.0 (`put_only_max_vix`), blocking the only viable alternative. On 05-29, VIX was 15.24 throughout — still above 15.0 — so the same gate prevented any put-only salvage across all 5 skip events. The put side on 05-28 had $140.00 credit at E#1 (well above the $1.25 Regime 0 floor), suggesting put premium was available and viable at the 10:45 entry time frame; the gate prevented capturing it at 11:15.

**Proposed change:** Raise `put_only_max_vix` from `15.0` to `16.5` in config. This would allow put-only fallback in VIX 15.0–16.5 range when call credit is below floor.

**Expected impact:** On 05-28 E#2, put-only would have been attempted. Based on E#1 put credit ($140.00), E#2 put credit was likely lower (HERMES noted ~$115 put credit at E#2 on 05-27 under similar VIX) but likely above the $1.25 floor. On 05-29, with VIX 15.24–15.55, put-only might have captured one viable entry. Estimated: 1-2 additional entries per week in sustained Regime 0 + trending sessions. At typical E#2 put credit of $100–$140 in this regime, this represents $100–$140 potential gross credit per week currently foregone.

**Risk:** MKT-032's 15.0 VIX ceiling exists because at low VIX, even put-only entries face mean-reversion risk on call side. However, call-only is already handled by MKT-040 in the opposite scenario. The asymmetry in the current config — call-only fallback works at any VIX, put-only is capped at VIX <15.0 — is difficult to justify given that 05-28 demonstrated put premium was available at VIX 16.0.

**Confidence: MEDIUM.** Insufficient historical data on put-only entries in VIX 15–17 range to quantify stop rate. The change should be deployed as a config parameter adjustment and monitored over 20+ trigger days before permanent adoption.

**Config change:** `strategy.put_only_max_vix: 15.0` → `strategy.put_only_max_vix: 16.5`

**Data basis:** On both 05-28 and 05-29, HYDRA executed 0–1 entries on days where SPX trended monotonically higher from open. The commission-per-viable-entry ratio becomes punishing at 1 entry/day: $10 commission on a $247.50 credit = 4% drag on maximum potential profit. The 05-28 and 05-29 pattern (gap-up open + VIX near 15–16 + monotonic drift upward) is a specific market regime not currently flagged by any HYDRA gate. Downday-035 and Upday-035 handle the 14:00 conditional but don't affect the base entry quality problem.

**Proposed consideration:** Evaluate adding a `trending_session_early_exit: true` config option — if by 10:30 ET the intraday move is already ≥0.20% from open AND VIX is below 16.0 (signaling Regime 0 compression risk), allow HYDRA to skip E#1 entirely and wait for E#2. This is a different mechanism from MKT-043 (which only delays by 5 min on rapid moves).

**Confidence: LOW.** This week has only 2 days of this pattern, insufficient to establish statistical significance. The change could increase daily skip counts on trending days while missing profitable entries on "false alarm" early moves that reverse by 10:45. Flagging as an observation for strategy memory only.

## Part 4: New Learnings for Strategy Memory

### W23 Key Findings (2026-05-26 through 2026-05-29):

1. **Regime 0 + monotonic uptrend = systematic skip cascade:** On both 05-28 and 05-29 (VIX 15.24–16.39), HYDRA completed only 1 entry and 0 entries respectively against a 2-entry base schedule. The mechanism: an upward-drifting SPX session compresses call-side premium below the $1.00 Regime 0 floor by E#2 (11:15), while VIX remaining above 15.0 blocks MKT-032's put-only fallback (put_only_max_vix: 15.0). Entry #3 (Upday-035) fires as put-only but finds $0 put premium in VIX-compressed environment. This cascade requires all three conditions simultaneously: (a) Regime 0 with $1.00 call floor, (b) upward SPX drift >0.3% by 11:00 ET, (c) VIX > 15.0.

2. **MKT-032 `put_only_max_vix: 15.0` gate blocks put-only fallback throughout most of VIX Regime 0 in practice:** At VIX 15.24 (05-29) and VIX 16.0 (05-28 E#2), the put-only gate was closed on both occasions despite put credit being available and viable. Since Regime 0 spans VIX < 18.0, the put-only gate (15.0) only permits fallback in the bottom quarter of the regime range. On 05-28, E#1 put credit was $140 (well above $1.25 floor), confirming put premium was accessible — but the gate prevented capturing it at E#2 time.

3. **The $1.00 Regime 0 call credit floor produces more skips at SPX 7,500+ than at historical calibration levels:** On 05-29 (SPX open 7,591.85), all three E#1/E#2 entry attempts were MKT-011 credit-gate failures on the call side. At 2.5× MKT-024 OTM from 7,592, short calls land near 7,630–7,636 (~40–44pt OTM) where VIX 15.24 generates sub-$1.00 credit on 75pt-wide spreads. The $1.00 Regime 0 floor was tuned at lower SPX index levels — the absolute dollar floor has not been adjusted for the index's ~10–15% appreciation since April 2026.

4. **Apollo's highest-quality calls of W23 occurred when it explicitly named the MKT rule binding constraint:** The 05-29 briefing correctly identified MKT-032's `put_only_max_vix: 15.0` as the binding gate and predicted the full skip cascade before market open — all 5 skip events occurred exactly as forecast. In contrast, the 05-28 GREEN call with "MKT-011 skips unlikely" was inaccurate because it did not condition on the trending-session call-premium compression dynamic. Apollo accuracy is materially higher when it simulates the specific MKT gate math rather than issuing regime-level generalizations.

5. **Four consecutive -$10 to -$20 commission-only days (05-22, 05-26, 05-27, 05-28) represent a distinct HYDRA failure mode:** These are technically "wins" in the stop/no-stop sense (zero stops on all four days) but accumulate $60 in commission drag on $0 net premium captured (all Expired Credits fields show $0 despite credits being placed). The winning-days count does not distinguish between "ICs expired with credit captured" and "ICs expired with credits offset by commissions." The cumulative losing streak (L15 as of 05-29) includes these commission-drag days as losses, which may overstate the structural severity of the current drawdown relative to a pure "stop days only" analysis.
