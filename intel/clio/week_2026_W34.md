<learnings>

- **OPEX-week Friday in Regime 0 at SPX 7,660–7,700 produces systematic put-premium collapse to $0.00 across all 7 B-grid slots (Aug 21, VIX 15.42, SPX open 7,672.38):** Put credits were $0.05 at Entries #1–#3 and literally $0.00 at Entries #4–#7, despite call credits remaining technically viable ($0.10–$0.43 on Entries #1–#4). HYDRA/Brandon has no OPEX-aware fallback; a cascade-skip trigger when put credit < $0.10 at Entry #2 on OPEX Fridays would prevent unnecessary evaluation of Entries #3–#7.

- **Brandon's early-close mechanisms (TP@80% + GEX breach exit) generated $2,285 in aggregate early-close debits against $3,630 gross credit in W34 (Aug 17–21) — consuming 63% of gross credit — while $0.00 in traditional stops fired.** This is the first documented week where early-close costs exceeded 50% of gross credit with zero formal stops.
